# Decisions made without a PT (2026-11-10)

The first PT handover list had 49 questions. Delan asked for it to be cut down: keep section A (should these exercises be in the app at all) for a PT, and settle the rest from published guidance. This file records what was decided and why. Source codes (S1 to S19) are listed at the end of `REVIEW.md`. "Conservative default" means no source settled it, so the safer option was taken.

None of this is medically verified. It is consistent with the cited sources.

## Tags, levels, names, claims

- **Tags.** 41 exercises lost tags that pointed people at them for a complaint without support, or against their own caution. Examples: bend-forward and hamstring stretches for back and leg discomfort, wrist stretches and the arm slider for hand and wrist strain, gripping and hammer curls for outer elbow soreness, gym lifts tagged for hip, back, shoulder, and thoracic outlet complaints, loaded carries tagged for balance. Rule used: when unsure, remove the tag.
- **Levels.** 21 exercises were re-rated. Now 147 Gentle, 126 Moderate, 39 Advanced.
- **Names.** Seven exercises lost diagnosis or method words (Tennis Elbow, Golfer's Elbow, Achilles, McKenzie, Piriformis). The old names still work in search.
- **Claims.** Removed or softened: "the medicine", "rebuilds the tendon", "research-backed", "wonderful for tension headaches", isometric holds "calm" pain, bike "easier on the knees than walking", backward walking "good for knees", "torches extra calories", "half the session time", "predicts independence", and similar. All were unverified.
- **Breath holding.** Deadlift and squat tips now say to breathe out as you lift. Box and 4-7-8 breathing say to skip the holds with a heart or lung condition or in pregnancy. Conservative default (S19 did not cover it).
