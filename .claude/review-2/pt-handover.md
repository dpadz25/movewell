# MoveWell: 9 questions for a licensed physical therapist

MoveWell is a free exercise companion app used mostly by older adults, on their own, with no clinician in the loop. It offers general exercises and ready-made routines. It must not diagnose or prescribe.

Everything that could be settled from published guidance has been settled and built (see `decisions.md`). What is left are judgment calls about whether specific exercises belong in an app nobody supervises. For each one please answer **keep**, **change** (say how), or **remove**. One line each is plenty.

Until you answer, every exercise below is out of all ready-made routines. People can still find them by browsing the exercise library.

## Should these be in an unsupervised app?

1. **First Rib Stretch (Towel).** A self-mobilization with a towel over the shoulder while tilting the head away.

2. **Two neck stretches that use end-range positions.**
   - Front-of-Neck (SCM) Stretch: head back, tilted, and turned together.
   - Scalene Stretch: side bend plus looking slightly up.
   Both are now rated Moderate and tell people to stop if dizzy.

3. **Arm and thigh nerve glides** (Median, Ulnar, Radial, Femoral). As written, three of them finish in a tension position, not a slide. They now say: light tension only, stop if tingling, numbness, or weakness appears or lasts.

4. **Nerve sliders** (Sciatic Nerve Floss seated, Lying Sciatic Nerve Glide, Neck and Arm Nerve Slider). These are written as true sliders. The seated one is still in the Back and Leg Mobility routine. Is the stop rule above enough, or should sliders be clinician-taught only?

5. **Back bending** (Press-Up, Lying on Elbows for 2 minutes, Standing Back Bend). All "centralization" and disc wording is gone. Each now says "skip this if standing or walking brings on leg pain", and the standing one says to keep a counter behind you. Is that enough for older adults who may have narrowing of the spine?

6. **Sleeper Stretch.** Now rated Moderate. Keep, or remove in favor of the Cross-Body Stretch?

7. **Two yoga backbends** (Fish Pose with weight toward the crown of the head, Camel Pose with the head back). Both are now rated Advanced. Fish points to a rolled-blanket version as the safer option.

8. **Harder balance work for people who chose "steadier balance"** (Eyes-Closed Stand, Grapevine Walk, Backward Walking). They now require a counter or wall, and the eyes-closed one says to have someone nearby. Right wording, or remove for this group?

## One wording check

9. **Emergency signs.** The app's "When to stop and get help" screen lists these as reasons to call 911: chest pain or pressure; new or unusual trouble breathing; fainting; sudden face drooping, arm weakness, or trouble speaking; back pain with numbness around the groin, trouble peeing, or weakness in both legs. Anything missing or wrong for people doing exercise at home?
