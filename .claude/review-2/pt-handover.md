# MoveWell: questions for a licensed physical therapist

MoveWell is a free exercise companion app used mostly by older adults, on their own, with no clinician in the loop. It offers general exercises and ready-made routines. It must not diagnose or prescribe.

An AI review flagged the items below as clinical judgment calls. Please mark each one **keep**, **change** (say how), or **remove**. Short answers are fine. Exercise names are as shown in the app.

## A. Should these be in an unsupervised app at all?

1. **First Rib Stretch (Towel).** A self-mobilization with a towel over the shoulder.
2. **Front-of-Neck (SCM) Stretch.** Head back, tilted, and turned together.
3. **Scalene Stretch.** Side bend plus looking up.
4. **Median, Ulnar, and Radial Nerve Glides.** As written they end in a tension position. The median glide says to tilt the head away "for a touch more glide".
5. **Femoral Nerve Glide.** Side-lying, heel to buttock with chin tuck.
6. **Seated and lying sciatic nerve sliders, and the Neck and Arm Nerve Slider.** Written as true sliders. Is "stop if tingling, numbness, or weakness increases or lasts" a sufficient stop rule?
7. **Press-Up and Lying on Elbows (2 minutes).** We plan to remove all "centralization" and disc wording and add "skip if standing or walking brings on leg pain". Is that enough, or should they go?
8. **Standing Back Bend.** Same question, plus the balance risk of leaning back.
9. **Sleeper Stretch.** Keep as a Moderate option, or remove in favor of the Cross-Body Stretch?
10. **Fish Pose** (weight toward the crown of the head) and **Camel Pose** for older adults.
11. **Eyes-Closed Stand, Grapevine Walk, Backward Walking** for people who chose "steadier balance". What support wording do you want?

## B. Doses and pain rules

12. **Slow heel lowering (heel drop).** App: 3 x 12 per side, once. The published program is 3 x 15, twice daily, straight and bent knee, 12 weeks. What dose and frequency suit an unsupervised older adult? Should the floor version be the default? Should people with soreness right at the heel bone avoid dropping below the step?
13. **"Some ache is normal if it settles by the next day."** Keep? If so, what number out of 10 is the ceiling, and does it apply to the wrist lowering exercises too?
14. **Wrist lowering exercises (outer and inner elbow).** 3 x 12 per side with 1 to 2 lb. How often per week?
15. **Wrist Extension Hold.** 5 x 30 s at medium effort. Reasonable first dose?
16. **Low back core trio.** Curl-Up 3 x 6 with 8 s holds, Side Plank (knees) 3 x 15 s per side, Bird Dog 3 x 8 with 5 s holds per side.
17. **Plantar Fascia Stretch.** 3 x 30 s per side. The trial used 10 x 10 s, three times a day.
18. **How often** should each rehab-style routine be done? The app currently says nothing and rewards daily streaks.

## C. Routine contents

19. **Back and Leg Mobility** contains both Knee to Chest and Press-Up. We plan to remove both and keep the nerve slider, Figure-4, bridge, and bird dog. Agree?
20. **Low Back Care** adds Knee to Chest and Lower Trunk Rotation to the core trio. Keep the stretches?
21. **Neck, Chest and Shoulder Opener** (formerly the thoracic outlet routine). We plan to remove the first rib move and both nerve glides, leaving stretches, chin tucks, rows, and shoulder-blade work. Agree?
22. **Wrist and Hand Care** serves hand and wrist strain, wrist soreness, and stiff fingers with one list, including a median nerve glide, a wrist flexor stretch, and the Prayer Stretch. What belongs?
23. **Elbow and Forearm Strength** loads both sides of the forearm and includes Grip Squeeze. Is gripping appropriate for outer elbow soreness?
24. **Foot & Heel Care** serves both arch soreness and heel cord soreness. Split into two?
25. **Steady & Strong (Balance).** Sit to stand, weight shifts, marching, side stepping, single-leg stand, tandem stance, heel-to-toe walking, heel raises, toe raises. About 29 minutes. Right order and length for a first balance routine?
26. **Gentle Full-Body Start and Seated Strength and Stretch** are offered to people who pick "getting moving after surgery". Is a single confirm ("my surgeon or therapist said I can exercise") an adequate gate?

## D. Which exercise suits which complaint (tags)

Should these stay linked to the complaint shown?

27. Knee to Chest, Hamstring Stretch with Strap, Pigeon Stretch, Glute Ball Release: back and leg discomfort.
28. Wrist Flexor Stretch, Prayer Stretch, Neck and Arm Nerve Slider: hand and wrist strain.
29. Grip Squeeze, Hammer Curl: outer elbow soreness.
30. Straight Leg Raise: "after surgery" in general.
31. Leg Extension machine: knee aches and stiff knees.
32. Seated and standing calf raise machines (full heel drop): heel cord soreness.
33. Shoulder Circles (CARs): stiff, limited shoulder.
34. Kettlebell Deadlift, 45-degree Back Extension: low back aches.

## E. Safety wording

35. **Osteoporosis.** We plan "skip, or keep your back straight and tip from the hips" on forward folds, crunches, sit-ups, the Curl-Up, and rowing, and "skip the arm lever" on seated twists. Gentle prone extension stays allowed. Agree?
36. **Hip replacement.** Is "follow the movement limits your surgeon gave you" enough on deep hip bends, crossed-leg stretches, and twists?
37. **Emergency signs** we plan to list: chest pain or pressure, unusual shortness of breath, fainting; back pain with numbness around the groin or bottom, trouble peeing or controlling bowels, or weakness in both legs; a painful, swollen, warm calf after surgery. Anything to add or reword (for example stroke signs, sudden severe headache)?
38. **See a professional soon** signs we plan to list: numbness, tingling, or weakness that spreads or lasts; pain at night or at rest; pain after a fall or injury; pain with fever or unexplained weight loss; no improvement after a few weeks. Changes?
39. **Pain score response.** We plan to show a rest-and-check-in message at 7 out of 10 or more, or a jump of 3 or more after a session. Right thresholds?
40. **One pain rule for the whole app.** "Stop if anything hurts" or "mild stretch or muscle effort is fine, sharp or spreading pain is not"? Your wording, please.
41. **Balance support.** "Hold a kitchen counter" as the standard, with "someone nearby" for eyes-closed work. Agree?
42. **Breath holds** in Box Breathing and 4-7-8 Breathing: who should skip the holds?
43. **Pregnancy.** Which positions or exercises need a note beyond "avoid long periods flat on your back"?

## F. Claims we could not verify

Please confirm or strike:

44. Isometric holds (Wall Sit, Wall Press, Wrist Extension Hold) "often calm" sore tendons or joints.
45. Backward walking is helpful for stiff knees.
46. The stationary bike is "often easier on the knees than walking".
47. Belly breathing "can help your neck and shoulder muscles relax".
48. Reaching the arms behind you counters a forward-tipped shoulder blade (exercise: Shoulder Extension).
49. "Frequent, gentle range-of-motion work" for a stiff shoulder, and "quad and hip strengthening" for knee aches, as general statements.
